# LiftFlow Subscription Terms

**Product:** LiftFlow Premium  
**Price:** $4.99 USD per month (local pricing may vary)  
**Apple Product ID:** `com.liftflow.app.premium.monthly`  
**Google Product ID:** `liftflow_premium_monthly`

## Auto-Renewable Subscription

LiftFlow Premium is an automatically renewing monthly subscription.

- **Length:** 1 month
- **Price:** $4.99/month (or equivalent in your App Store region)
- **Renewal:** Your subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period
- **Billing:** Your Apple ID or Google Play account is charged within 24 hours prior to renewal

## Premium Features Include

- AI coaching recommendations and refresh
- AI meal plan generation
- Physique projections and advanced body analysis
- Health & Strava integrations
- PDF export features

## Free Tier

Core workout logging, history, basic nutrition logging, and progress photos remain available on the free tier unless otherwise noted in-app.

## Cancellation

Cancel anytime:

- **iOS:** Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → LiftFlow
- **Android:** Google Play → Subscriptions → LiftFlow

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. No refund for partial periods except as required by law or store policy.

## Restore Purchases

Use **Restore Purchases** on the Subscription screen if you reinstall the app or switch devices. You must use the same Apple ID or Google account used for the original purchase.

## Refunds

Refund requests are handled by Apple or Google per their policies. Contact support@liftflow.app for assistance.

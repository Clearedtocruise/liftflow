## Description

LiftFlow is your AI-powered fitness companion for serious lifters and everyday athletes.

**Voice Workout Logging**  
Speak your sets naturally — "bench press 225 for 8" — and LiftFlow logs them instantly.

**Smart Coaching**  
Get recovery-aware recommendations based on your training volume, nutrition, and goals.

**Nutrition & Meal Plans**  
Track macros, log meals, and generate weekly meal plans tailored to your targets.

**Progress Tracking**  
Upload progress photos, track body composition, and export PDF reports.

**Integrations**  
Sync Apple Health, Health Connect, Strava, and Apple Watch data (Premium).

**Premium — $4.99/month**  
Unlock AI coaching refresh, meal plan generation, health sync, physique projections, and exports.

LiftFlow provides informational coaching only. Not medical advice. Always consult a professional before changing your training or nutrition program.

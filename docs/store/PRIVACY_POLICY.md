# LiftFlow Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** May 28, 2026  
**Contact:** support@liftflow.app

## Overview

LiftFlow ("we," "our," or "us") provides a fitness coaching mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## Information We Collect

- **Account information:** email address, display name, profile data you provide
- **Fitness data:** workouts, sets, nutrition logs, progress photos, goals, metrics
- **Health integrations (optional):** steps, weight, heart rate, workouts from Apple Health, Health Connect, or Strava when you connect those services
- **Voice data:** speech transcripts for workout logging (processed on-device and/or via our API; audio files are not stored unless you enable recording)
- **Device information:** device type, OS version, push notification tokens (when enabled)
- **Subscription data:** purchase status via Apple/Google (we do not store full payment card numbers)

## How We Use Information

- Provide workout tracking, AI coaching, nutrition features, and integrations you enable
- Sync data across your devices via Supabase
- Process subscriptions and restore purchases
- Improve app reliability and support

## Third-Party Services

We use:

- **Supabase** — authentication and database hosting
- **Render** — backend API hosting
- **OpenAI** — optional AI coaching and parsing (when API key configured)
- **Apple / Google** — in-app purchases and app distribution
- **Strava** — optional activity import when you connect your account

## Data Retention & Deletion

You may delete your account in Settings → Delete Account. This removes your profile and associated data subject to legal retention requirements.

## Health Data

LiftFlow is not a medical device. Health data is used for informational coaching only. We do not sell health data.

## Children's Privacy

LiftFlow is not intended for users under 13.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
